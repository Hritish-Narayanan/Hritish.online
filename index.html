A privacy-oriented multi-chain wallet. Keys never touch the internet. Signing happens air-gapped. Traffic routes through Tor. BTC → XMR → ETH, and the trail goes cold.

Solo, open-source. BIP-39/BIP-32 HD key management, SHA-512 → AES-256-GCM local encryption, modular swap engine for privacy-preserving chain hops. Sepolia testnet demo included. Under construction — the good parts always are.

A Firefox extension that reads Google Forms, sends the questions to an LLM, and fills in the answers. In Docs, it becomes an AI writing assistant.

Extracts questions and candidate answers, routes them through the z.ai API, and completes the form. In Google Docs it offers AI-powered writing assistance — content generation and suggestions, in place, without leaving the page.
